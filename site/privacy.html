Privacy at Vraxis Drop

Last updated: 12 October 2026. This page describes the current beta implementation. It is not a claim of independent security certification.

What you share

Quick Drop stores the text or file you submit temporarily on Supabase infrastructure so the recipient can retrieve it using the pickup code. Standard Quick Drops are not end-to-end encrypted. Private Mode encrypts the content in your browser before upload; anyone holding the complete private link may retrieve it.

Retention

Quick Drops become inaccessible through their codes after one pickup or after your selected one-hour or 24-hour expiry. Expired storage objects are removed opportunistically; physical deletion is not guaranteed at the exact expiry time. Backups and provider logs may persist separately.

Connect

Connect uses temporary signaling information to establish a browser-to-browser WebRTC connection. The current setup uses public STUN and does not guarantee a connection on every network. Connection metadata and signaling may pass through our backend; transferred content travels over the WebRTC data channel when a connection succeeds.

Accounts and purchases

Free transfers do not require an account. Optional sign-in uses your email address through Supabase Auth. Purchase records may include billing email, provider customer identifiers, plan and status supplied by Lemon Squeezy. Paid transfers are not yet available.

Security and abuse prevention

We process network information such as IP addresses for rate limiting and security. Transfer endpoints use short-lived access codes and server-side limits. We cannot promise absolute security; avoid sharing highly sensitive material through standard drops.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the website, Supabase supports authentication, storage and backend processing, and Lemon Squeezy is configured for potential future payments. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

Requests and corrections

Before launch, the operator must publish a verified privacy-contact email and establish a process for access, deletion and other applicable privacy requests. Until then, do not represent this page as a complete legal privacy notice.
