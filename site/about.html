Our story

Less friction.

Vraxis Drop exists for the tiny moments that shouldn't require a whole app.

Why we built this

Sharing should feel effortless.

We've all been there: a link on your phone that you need on your laptop, a quick note you want on another screen, or a file that should be easy to move. Vraxis Drop offers a simple answer: create a temporary drop, share its code, and pick it up wherever you need it.

It's intentionally accountless. No feed, no profile, no extra steps. Just a focused space for short-lived transfers.

Designed to disappear.

Drops have short availability windows, and each pickup code can be claimed once. The current version is an evolving prototype: it is not end-to-end encrypted, and automated cleanup of expired files is still being completed. We believe clarity about those limits matters.

Made for real life.

Vraxis Drop is built to work across modern browsers and screen sizes. We're refining the experience with better accessibility, faster transfers, and thoughtful details that make every interaction feel natural.
