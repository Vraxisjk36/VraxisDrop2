Using Vraxis Drop

Last updated: 12 October 2026. These beta terms are a draft and require operator and legal review before a commercial launch.

What the service does

Vraxis Drop lets you transfer temporary text and files using Quick Drop or connect two browsers using Connect. Transfers are subject to the limits shown on the plans page. Paid plans are not for sale or active.

Your responsibilities

Only share content you are authorized to share. Do not use the service for illegal content, harassment, malware distribution, unauthorized access or attempts to bypass usage limits. Keep pickup codes and private links confidential.

Availability

This is a beta service provided without an uptime guarantee. Network restrictions, device limitations, rate limits or maintenance may interrupt transfers. Connect may fail without a compatible direct WebRTC route. Keep your own backup of important files.

Temporary content

Quick Drop codes expire after the selected period or first claim, but backend cleanup of expired objects can occur later. A successful pickup consumes a code even if a subsequent download fails; do not use Vraxis Drop as permanent storage.

Privacy and security

See our privacy page. Standard Quick Drops are not end-to-end encrypted. Private Mode provides browser-side encryption but has not undergone independent security review.

Before commercial launch

The operator must add the legal business identity, contact details, applicable governing law, consumer rights, refunds, cancellation rules and payment terms before accepting paid orders.

Collect beta limits

Collect links allow anonymous uploads to a verified account holder. Each collection expires after seven days and accepts at most 20 files and 50 MB total, with a 10 MB limit per file. A collection link is a submission capability: share it only with intended contributors. Do not upload harmful, illegal or unauthorized content.
